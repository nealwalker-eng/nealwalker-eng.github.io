Who We Are

America Protective Security (APS) is a dedicated small business delivering essential security services to government and commercial clients, with a focus on ethical practices, operational excellence, and integrity. Drawing on over 20 years of experience from government and private sectors, we maintain stringent certifications and policies for federal compliance. As we expand, APS cultivates a culture of safety and reliability, establishing us as a trusted partner in protecting federal properties and commercial assets.

What We Do

APS delivers comprehensive security solutions to mitigate risks and safeguard assets in varied environments, including armed and unarmed personnel, static and mobile patrols, command center operations, and advanced access control systems. Tailored for government facilities—ensuring compliance with 41 CFR Part 102-74 and jurisdictional laws—and commercial enterprises, our services prevent disruptions and promote business continuity. By combining skilled officers with innovative technology, we offer layered protection that deters threats, enables swift incident response, and upholds regulatory standards while advancing client goals.

Our Security Services

America Protective Security provides comprehensive security solutions for government and commercial clients, upholding the highest standards of protection and compliance.

Government Security

Our government security services are designed to protect federal properties, adhering to jurisdictional guidelines such as exclusive, concurrent, and proprietary jurisdictions. We deploy armed and unarmed officers for static posts, mobile patrols, vigilant monitoring, access control, and rapid incident response, in line with FPS and DHS protocols. This includes detainment under state laws, assault prevention per 18 U.S.C. §111, and facility conduct rules from 41 CFR Part 102-74, positioning APS as a dependable partner for agencies requiring compliant protection.

Commercial Security

For commercial clients, APS offers flexible security solutions to protect businesses, assets, and personnel from emerging threats. Services include entry screening, perimeter patrols, and command center integration, customized for corporate campuses, healthcare facilities, and transportation hubs. Through proactive risk assessment and operational support, we enhance productivity while reducing vulnerabilities, adapting federal best practices for superior protection.

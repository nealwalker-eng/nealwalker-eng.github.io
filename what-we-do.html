What We Do

APS provides tailored, integrated security services that address complex risks for government and commercial clients, focusing on proactive threat mitigation, regulatory compliance, and seamless operational support to safeguard people, assets, and infrastructure.

Our portfolio includes armed and unarmed security, static and mobile patrols, command center operations, and access control, all designed to deter threats and ensure continuity. For government needs, we emphasize federal jurisdiction compliance and assault prevention; for commercial, we prioritize asset protection and incident response.

**What's Left to Complete Deployment:**

To make your website live, you'll need to enable GitHub Pages in your repository settings. Simply go to your GitHub repository, navigate to Settings → Pages, and select "GitHub Actions" as the source. Once enabled, the automated deployment process will begin, and your website will be live at https://nealwalker-eng.github.io/ within 5-10 minutes. This is a one-time setup that takes less than a minute to complete.

Government Security

Our Focus on Government Security in the NCR

At America Protective Security (APS), we are dedicated to enhancing security for the approximately 688 federal buildings managed by the GSA in the National Capital Region (NCR), within a 50-mile radius of Washington, DC. This inventory includes federally owned and leased properties totaling about 92 million square feet, supporting over 300,000 federal workers across offices, courthouses, and administrative facilities. Through high-quality physical security services and strategic partnerships, we deliver compliant, tailored solutions that safeguard critical assets, making the NCR safer and more secure for these buildings and personnel.

Detailed Services

Armed/Unarmed Static

Officers stationed at fixed posts conduct entry screening, surveillance, and access control, compliant with 41 CFR Part 102-74 for conduct on federal property. This includes recognizing crimes, managing detainment under state laws (e.g., private person arrest), and preventing assaults per 18 U.S.C. §111.

Mobile Patrols

Vehicle-based units perform facility sweeps, incident response, and visibility patrols in exclusive, concurrent, or proprietary jurisdictions, integrating with command centers for real-time coordination.

Command Center

24/7 monitoring with alarm response, video feeds, and dispatch, utilizing technologies like biometric systems and sensor integration to meet FISMA and HSPD-12 standards.

Applications

Ideal for federal buildings, DOE/NRC sites, and agency facilities; includes risk assessments, post orders, and training aligned with PSOTP for handling threats in high-security zones.

Benefits

  • Provides layered defense that deters threats
  • Ensures compliance with U.S.C. §111 for protecting federal employees
  • Maintains operational continuity through vetted, licensed officers
  • Reduces vulnerabilities through proactive security measures
  • Enhances accountability via comprehensive incident reporting
  • Supports federal procurement goals as a small business

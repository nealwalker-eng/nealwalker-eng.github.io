Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between armed and unarmed security guards?

Unarmed security guards focus on prevention, deterrence, access control, and customer service without carrying weapons. They're ideal for retail, offices, residential properties, and low-risk environments where a professional presence and excellent communication skills are the priority.

Are your unarmed security guards licensed?

Yes, all APS unarmed security guards hold valid licenses in Maryland (MD License: 106-4499), Washington DC (DC License: SAB3178), or Virginia (VA DCJS: 11-4726) depending on their assignment location.

What training do your unarmed security officers receive?

Our unarmed security guards complete comprehensive training in access control, emergency procedures, de-escalation techniques, customer service, report writing, CPR/First Aid, and professional communication skills.

How much do unarmed security guards cost?

Unarmed security guard rates vary based on location (Maryland, DC, or Virginia), hours required, and specific duties. Contact us for a customized quote tailored to your security needs and budget.

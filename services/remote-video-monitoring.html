Frequently Asked Questions

How is remote video monitoring different from regular security cameras?

Regular cameras just record footage. Remote video monitoring has live operators watching your cameras 24/7, detecting threats in real-time, issuing audio warnings, and dispatching police immediately. It's proactive security, not just reactive recording.

What happens when a threat is detected?

Our operators instantly review the alert, verify if it's a real threat, issue an audio warning through speakers, and if the intruder doesn't leave, we dispatch local police with verified video evidence. Average response time is under 30 seconds.

How much does remote video monitoring cost?

Remote monitoring typically costs 50-70% less than on-site security guards while providing better coverage. Pricing varies based on number of cameras, property size, and monitoring level. Contact us for a customized quote for Maryland, DC, or Virginia.

Do I need to buy new cameras?

We can work with many existing camera systems or provide new AI-powered cameras as part of our service. Our team will assess your current setup and recommend the best solution for your Maryland, DC, or Virginia property.

Can I access my cameras remotely?

Yes! You'll have 24/7 access to live and recorded video through our mobile app and web portal. View cameras, receive alerts, and communicate with our monitoring center from anywhere.

Is remote video monitoring legal in Maryland, DC, and Virginia?

Yes, remote video monitoring is completely legal in all three jurisdictions when proper signage is posted. APS ensures full compliance with Maryland, DC, and Virginia surveillance and privacy laws.

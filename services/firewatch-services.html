Frequently Asked Questions

What is fire watch and when is it required?

Fire watch is the assignment of trained personnel to monitor for fires when fire protection systems are impaired or during hot work. OSHA requires fire watch when sprinklers, alarms, or other fire systems are non-functional, and during welding, cutting, or other hot work operations in Maryland, DC, and Virginia.

How quickly can you deploy fire watch guards?

We offer same-day fire watch deployment throughout Maryland, DC, and Virginia. For emergency situations, we can typically have certified guards on-site within 2-4 hours. Call 1-800-APS-SEC for immediate fire watch service.

Are your fire watch guards OSHA certified?

Yes! All APS fire watch guards complete OSHA-compliant training covering fire hazard recognition, fire extinguisher use, emergency response procedures, patrol protocols, and documentation requirements per OSHA 1910.252 and 1926.352 standards.

How much does fire watch service cost?

Fire watch rates vary based on location (Maryland, DC, or Virginia), shift length, number of guards needed, and duration of service. We offer competitive hourly rates with discounts for multi-day projects. Contact us for a customized quote.

Do fire watch guards patrol or stay in one location?

Fire watch guards conduct regular patrols throughout the property every 30-60 minutes (or as required by OSHA/local fire marshal). They inspect all areas, document observations, and maintain continuous monitoring for fire hazards and emergencies.

What documentation do fire watch guards provide?

Our guards maintain detailed patrol logs with timestamps, observations, hazards identified, corrective actions taken, and incident reports. These documents demonstrate OSHA compliance and are provided to clients daily for record-keeping and inspections.

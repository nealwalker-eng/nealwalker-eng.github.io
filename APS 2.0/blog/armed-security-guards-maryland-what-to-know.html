Individual Guard Requirements

Armed security guards in Maryland must obtain:

  • Wear & Carry Permit: Maryland Handgun Permit specifically for security work
  • Security Guard License: State-issued credential after background check
  • Training Certification: Minimum 40 hours initial training plus 8 hours annual renewal
  • Firearms Qualification: Regular range qualifications demonstrating proficiency
  • Background Check: FBI fingerprint-based criminal history review

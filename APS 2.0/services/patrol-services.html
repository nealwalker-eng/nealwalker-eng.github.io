Frequently Asked Questions

How often should mobile patrols visit my property?

Patrol frequency depends on your security needs. Common schedules include hourly checks, 2-4 visits per shift, or randomized patrols. We customize patrol schedules for Maryland, DC, and Virginia properties based on risk assessment and budget.

Are mobile patrols available 24/7?

Yes, APS provides 24/7 mobile security patrol services throughout Maryland, Washington DC, and Virginia. We offer daytime, nighttime, weekend, and holiday coverage.

What happens if patrol officers find a security issue?

Officers immediately report any security concerns, contact designated personnel, and can contact local authorities if needed. Detailed incident reports with photos and timestamps are provided.

How much do mobile patrol services cost?

Mobile patrol service costs vary based on patrol frequency, property size, location (Maryland, DC, or Virginia), and specific requirements. Contact us for a customized quote.

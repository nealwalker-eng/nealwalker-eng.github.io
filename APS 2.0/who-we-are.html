Our History

Founded in 2010 as a small, service-disabled, minority-owned, Army veteran-led security provider in the Washington, DC Metro region, APS has expanded across the DMV area through strategic growth and partnerships. Beginning with commercial contracts, we progressed into federal protective services through the implementation of FPS-compliant training programs. Key milestones include the launch of mobile patrols in Maryland and Virginia in 2024 and securing our first major government contract in 2025. Drawing inspiration from industry leaders, APS integrates innovation with ethical integrity, establishing itself as a trusted provider in physical security for federal and commercial clients.

Chief Bajon Bihai

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Chief Bajon Bihai is a distinguished veteran and the visionary founder of America Protective Security. With 15 years of comprehensive experience in the security industry, Chief Bihai has built APS from the ground up into a trusted regional leader in protective services.

As a service-disabled Army veteran, Chief Bihai brings military precision, strategic leadership, and an unwavering commitment to excellence to every aspect of APS operations. His extensive background spans both commercial and federal security sectors, enabling him to develop innovative solutions that meet the highest standards of safety and professionalism.

Under his leadership, APS has achieved significant milestones including FPS compliance, expansion across the DMV region, and securing major government contracts. Chief Bihai's vision continues to guide the company's strategic growth and commitment to protecting critical assets with uncompromising integrity.
